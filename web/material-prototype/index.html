Hypotheses you are testing

  • Every agent has the same properties and graph format. A resource-like preset simply has all arrows leading to Wait.
  • Repair converts reserve into structure. Wear converts structure into loose material; it does not change the graph or delete reserve at zero structure.
  • Acquisition transfers another agent’s loose material; it cannot reclaim your own loose material.
  • Copy transfers 5 reserve into a child: 3 structure + 2 reserve. Attempt wear is 2. Children and separated fragments inherit the source graph.
  • Move carries all material and has attempt wear of 1. Crowding uses a proposed material capacity.
  • No automatic recycling, refilling, age limit or background ticking. Zero structure is not a blanket action ban. Steps are manual, not lifetimes.

Still open: acquisition as a separate unit action or part of Repair, occupancy/capacity, and access to held loose material. Simultaneous competing claims, autonomous probability adjustment and inherited mutation are not represented here.
